## Rules for modified and third-party distributions

Unless the applicable rights holder has given prior written permission, a
modified, forked, or third-party distribution must:

1. remove or replace the Nagram name, logos, and application icons used as
   product branding;
2. use a clearly distinct application name and icon;
3. remove Nagram brand assets from icon selectors, application bundles,
   manifests, release packages, store listings, screenshots, repository
   avatars, and promotional material; and
4. not suggest affiliation with, endorsement by, or official status from the
   Nagram project.

Accurate, non-confusing source attribution such as "based on Nagram-iOS" is
permitted. Required copyright notices, source history, and open-source license
notices must remain intact.

## Identified Nagram application icon assets

This policy applies to the Nagram-specific artwork represented by these path
families:

- `Telegram/Telegram-iOS/Nagram*.icon/**`
- `Telegram/Telegram-iOS/Nagram*.alticon/**`
- `Telegram/Telegram-iOS/Nagram*.png`

The current artwork families include Default Nagram, Block, Block Blue, Block
Niello, Classic, and Colorful. `Build` and `26` suffixed resources are packaging
or toolchain variants of those families. This policy continues to apply when
the artwork is copied, renamed, moved, recolored, adapted, or incorporated into
another resource.

These paths identify Nagram brand assets only. Generic Telegram assets and
third-party artwork in nearby directories remain subject to their own rights,
licenses, and trademark policies.
